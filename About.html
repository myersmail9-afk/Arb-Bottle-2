Cache Valley, Utah · est. 2024

We don't sell bottles. We sell the trip back.

A bottle is just stainless until it carries something — a ridgeline you climbed, a name you love, the coordinates of a day you'll never forget.

Lost Mountain started with a frustration: every engraving shop online looked the same — a logo uploader, a three-day wait, an emailed proof you couldn't really see. The machine had become a commodity. The taste hadn't.

So we built the thing we wished existed. A bottle that floats in front of you, that you can spin and zoom and actually feel before you buy. A vectorizer that turns your photo into laser-ready art the instant you drop it. A studio that takes five photos from a trip and composes them into one drawing worth keeping forever.

None of this was possible to build solo a year ago. The interface, the live 3D, the on-device image processing, the AI composition — this is a small outdoor company punching at a scale that used to require a team of ten. That's the whole point.
