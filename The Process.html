The Process

A fiber laser doesn't print — it permanently marks the steel itself. That's why a Lost Mountain bottle never peels, fades, or scratches off. Here's the whole path, from upload to your hands.

01

Design

Draw it, photograph it, or build it in Trip Studio. Two-tone is the language of the laser.

02

Vectorize

Your browser converts the image to clean laser paths in real time — no design software, no proof emails.

03

Place

Preview the art on the live 3D bottle — wrap the full 360° or keep it to one face. What you spin is what gets made.

04

Engrave

The file routes to the laser. We mark, clean, and inspect every bottle by hand.

05

Ship

Packed in a kraft tube with a coordinate card, on its way in five days.

Design for steel

What engraves beautifully — and what fights the laser.

The laser sees contrast, not color. Bold and graphic wins. If you're not sure, the threshold slider on the bottle tells you instantly.

✓ Engraves clean

  • High contrast. Bold shapes and clear edges.Logos, silhouettes, line drawings, topo lines.
  • Single-line art. Sketches and signatures.Like the Lost Mountain mark itself.
  • Solid type. Medium-to-heavy weights.Coordinates, names, short phrases.
  • Strong photos. Clear subject, simple background.A ridgeline against sky, a portrait in good light.

✕ Fights the steel

  • Smooth gradients. No tonal range on metal.Sunsets and soft fades flatten out.
  • Hair-thin detail. Sub-millimeter lines drop out.Tiny serif text, fine crosshatch, watermarks.
  • Busy photos. Cluttered scenes turn to mud.Crowds, dense foliage, low light.
  • Color-dependent art. The laser is monochrome.Designs that only read in full color.
LIFETIME ENGRAVING GUARANTEE

304 stainless, double-wall vacuum insulation, fiber-laser engraving rated for the life of the bottle — wrapped over the entire surface, top to base, all the way around. If your mark ever fades, we re-do it free.
